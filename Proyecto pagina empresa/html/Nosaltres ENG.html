Who are we?

We are a company dedicated to solving hardware and software problems, also to the sale of general items with everything related to gamer computing that has a technical service both in the store and at home. Our strong point is diverse, we have the customization of the team to the complete taste of the customer, we do everything possible to meet the needs of our customers. We also have the annual service at a cost of (to be defined) which takes you through the monitoring of your latest maintenance so you can be notified when you need one. If you assemble or buy your gamer computer ready-made (or not, we also assemble for all kinds of public) with us you have a guarantee for life (for damages caused by our fault without manipulation by third parties).

What do we offer?

We offer retail personalization services, in multiple aspects such as custom liquid cooling, the assembly of computers in places that are not usual with an additional cost for travel to the home or wherever the computer needs to be mounted, that is, if you want the computer to be mounted somewhere in your home (to the customer's liking), we go to the home and do whatever the customer requests and is within our reach, we do the work and we bring our own materials and tools to achieve what the client asks for.

What do we know how to do?

We specialize in programming, design, office automation, assembly, maintenance, repair, software and system installation, database administration, Windows and Linux configuration (client and server), network maintenance, local network configuration and cyber security.
